# Revision history: Cycle Universality for a Single Thorp Shuffle

## Expanded revision 2

### Title and presentation

- Retitled the paper **Cycle Universality for a Single Thorp Shuffle**.
- Retained Christopher D. Long as sole author.
- Added a conventional mathematical introduction and abstract, native numbered theorem environments, stable equation labels, and an explicit distinction between the three Poisson/Gaussian regimes.
- Removed the review/version banner and audit discussion from the abstract. Moved the detailed computational record into these supporting documents; retained a short reproducibility paragraph in the paper.
- Removed conversion debris, normalized the earlier-cycle vector notation, and removed a duplicate scalar-limit display. The finished manuscript is 21 pages.

### Definition correction and proof clarifications

- The closing-query count K now includes a context first fixed by an earlier query in the same closing segment, as well as contexts fixed by an earlier marked cycle or the current open path. This is necessary for both the likelihood identity and J + K = m_new.
- Placed the definition before the likelihood formula.
- Expanded the integration chain after the root-averaged Jensen estimate and stated the re-rooting invariance of its sparse class.
- Explained why a reverse step queries the same feedback entry as the forward step into its current state.
- Stated that the lower bound depends on the exponent restriction through the already established one-cycle upper bound; its numerical constant is independent of that exponent.
- Made the initial [4d,b] restriction of the moment comparison explicit.

### Additional theorems and corollaries

- Bounded-harmonic-mass Poisson total-variation approximation, including growing coordinate sets. The finite factorial-moment inclusion–exclusion lemma is proved, with the moment order kept fixed before taking the dimension limit.
- Scale-invariant Poisson convergence for moving mesoscopic windows.
- Standard Dickman limit for normalized truncated cycle mass, with its Laplace transform specified.
- Uniform limit for the largest cycle below a prescribed cutoff.
- Quantitative birthday-range full-vector Poisson approximation, with its separate context-simple-necklace, sequential-forcing, entropy, and dependency-neighborhood proof imported explicitly.
- Exact short-cycle binomial laws and joint independence through floor((d+1)/2), with credit to Stark's earlier independent-binomial theorem.
- A general finite-family additive Gaussian corollary.
- Lower bounds with high probability for the full permutation order and total cycle count.
- Sharpened the expected product-to-LCM discrepancy to O(log b log log b), with the elementary Chebyshev estimate supplied.

The main factorial-moment range and normalization, and the truncated functional law for every fixed exponent below two thirds, are unchanged. No full permutation-order CLT or endpoint theorem has been added.
